# E-commerce Customer & Sales Analysis

## Executive Summary
The analysis covers **1,800 synthetic e-commerce orders** across **400 customers** during 2025.

- Revenue: **₹44,422,910**
- Profit: **₹9,959,939**
- Profit margin: **22.4%**
- Average order value: **₹24,679**

## Key Findings
- Revenue reached ₹44,422,910 across 1,800 orders, with total profit of ₹9,959,939 and an overall margin of 22.4%.
- Laptop generated the highest revenue at ₹18,867,145.
- Backpack had the strongest product-level margin at 43.7%.
- South generated the highest revenue at ₹12,441,558, while North had the lowest regional margin at 22.2%.
- Regular customers generated the most revenue at ₹19,833,672.
- The correlation between discount rate and realized profit margin was -0.54; higher discounts were associated with lower margins in this dataset.

## Recommendations
- Protect margins on heavily discounted orders: the highest discount band averaged 10.9% margin versus 29.7% for the lowest discount band.
- Prioritize Laptop for inventory and cross-sell campaigns because it is the largest revenue contributor.
- Investigate North pricing, discounts and product mix because it has the weakest regional margin.
- Use customer tiers to focus retention offers on High Value customers and targeted reactivation campaigns for At Risk customers.
- Test discount thresholds rather than broad discounts; measure incremental revenue against lost margin.

## Analysis Areas
- Revenue trend analysis
- Product profitability
- Regional performance
- Customer segmentation using revenue, frequency and recency
- Discount effectiveness
