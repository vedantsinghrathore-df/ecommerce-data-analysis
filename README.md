# E-commerce Customer & Sales Analysis

## Overview

A business analytics project analyzing e-commerce sales and customer data to identify revenue trends, product performance, profitability, customer segments, and opportunities for improvement.

## Project Highlights

- 1,800 e-commerce orders analyzed
- 400 customers
- Revenue and profit analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Regional profitability analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Discount impact analysis
- Business recommendations

## Key Results

- Total Revenue: ₹44.42M
- Total Profit: ₹9.96M
- Profit Margin: 22.4%
- Total Orders: 1,800
- Discount vs Profit Margin Correlation: -0.54

## Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented using:

- Revenue
- Purchase frequency
- Recency

Segments include:

- High Value
- Growing
- At Risk
- Low Engagement
